## How to check a used Toyota pickup before you buy

A few minutes of homework protects your money. Use these steps on any used truck, here or anywhere.

1. **Run the VIN for recalls.** Open recalls are free to fix at any Toyota dealer. Check the VIN at [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
2. **Check fair market value.** Compare the asking price to independent guides like [kbb.com](https://www.kbb.com) and [edmunds.com](https://www.edmunds.com) before you negotiate.
3. **Review the service history.** Consistent oil changes and maintenance records matter more on a truck that may have towed or hauled.
4. **Inspect the bed and frame.** Look for rust, weld repairs, and heavy towing wear. Our team does this on every truck we list.
5. **Ask about CPO eligibility.** Some used Toyotas qualify for Certified Used coverage, which changes the value math.

For independent reliability and ownership-cost reading on Toyota trucks, [consumerreports.org](https://www.consumerreports.org) is a solid second opinion.
